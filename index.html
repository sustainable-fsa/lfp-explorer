    About this map

    USDA FSA normal grazing periods · 2008–2026

    This map shows the normal grazing periods USDA's Farm Service Agency reported for each FSA county, pasture type, and program year. It is a research reference, not a determination: FSA county offices are the authoritative source for the dates that apply to a producer, and eligibility questions belong with them. Consult your local FSA office — find it with the USDA Service Center Locator.
